# UAE Immigration and Golden Visa — Overview

_Illustrative simplified summary for a search demo — not legal advice._
_Sourced from Federal Decree-Law No. 29 of 2021 on Entry and Residence
of Foreigners (in force 17 Sep 2021) and Cabinet Decision No. 65 of
2022, as summarized on the UAE Government's official platform (u.ae)._

Federal Decree-Law No. 29 of 2021 replaced the UAE's 1973 entry and
residence law and is the current general legal basis for foreigners'
entry, residence, and deportation in the UAE, implemented in more
practical detail through Cabinet Decision No. 65 of 2022. Most standard
work and family residence visas obtained through this framework are
employer- or family-sponsored and tied to that sponsorship, historically
requiring renewal roughly every 1-3 years and lapsing if the underlying
job or family relationship ends.

The "Golden Visa" is a long-term residence category created under this
framework for specific groups — investors, entrepreneurs, certain
professionals and scientists, outstanding students, and others named in
the implementing rules — granting residency for 5 or 10 years,
automatically renewable, without needing an employer or national
sponsor to maintain it. This is a structural difference from ordinary
sponsored visas: a Golden Visa holder generally keeps their status even
after changing or leaving a job, and can sponsor family members
(including spouses and children) more flexibly.

A further practical difference is absence tolerance: ordinary residence
visas can lapse if the holder stays outside the UAE longer than the
standard allowed period (commonly cited as around six months), while
Golden Visa holders are allowed longer absences without losing their
residency, reflecting the program's aim of attracting long-term
investment and talent rather than requiring continuous physical
presence.
