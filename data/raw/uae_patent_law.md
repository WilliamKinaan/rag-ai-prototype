# UAE Patent Law — Overview

_Illustrative simplified summary for a search demo — not legal advice._
_Sourced from Federal Law No. 11 of 2021 on the Regulation and
Protection of Industrial Property Rights, Ministry of Economy & Tourism
official materials and WIPO Lex._

Federal Law No. 11 of 2021 consolidates the UAE's industrial property
regime, covering not just patents but also utility certificates,
industrial designs, integrated circuit layout designs, and undisclosed
information (trade secrets) in a single statute rather than separate
laws for each category, which was the prior arrangement. It was part of
a large package of roughly 50 federal laws amended together, reflecting
a broader push to modernize the UAE's commercial and IP framework at
the same time.

A patent under this law protects a new invention that is industrially
applicable, while the law's separate "utility certificate" category
offers a faster, lower-bar alternative for incremental innovations that
may not clear the full inventive-step threshold required for a standard
patent — a two-tier structure aimed at covering both major breakthroughs
and smaller practical improvements. Applications are examined and
granted by the Ministry of Economy, which also maintains the relevant
registers, and protection is again territorial to the UAE rather than
automatically covering the wider Gulf region.

Industrial designs and trade secrets sit under the same law but follow
distinct protection logic: registered industrial designs protect the
visual appearance of a product for a fixed term, while trade secrets are
protected only for as long as the information is actually kept
confidential, with no registration process at all.
