INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Independent Contractor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into
between Acme Client LLC ("Client") and the individual or entity signing below
("Contractor").

1. Term
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and shall continue in effect
until either party decides to end it.

2. Services
Contractor shall provide software development services to Client as
reasonably requested from time to time.

3. Termination
Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no
reason, effective immediately upon written or verbal notice. Contractor may
not terminate this Agreement except for Client's uncured material breach,
and must provide 90 days' advance written notice before doing so.

4. Payment
Client shall pay Contractor's invoices within 120 days of receipt. No
interest or penalty shall accrue on any late payment, regardless of cause
or duration of delay.

5. Ownership of Work Product
Contractor will create various deliverables in the course of performing the
Services. Ownership of these deliverables will be determined at Client's
discretion at a later date.

6. Confidentiality
Contractor agrees to keep all information disclosed by Client confidential
forever, and shall not disclose it to any party under any circumstances,
including in response to a valid court order. Client shall have no
corresponding confidentiality obligations with respect to any information
Contractor discloses to Client.

7. Non-Competition
During the Term and for a period of ten (10) years thereafter, Contractor
shall not, anywhere in the world, provide software development services of
any kind to any individual or company, whether or not such services compete
with Client's business.

8. Limitation of Liability
Contractor's total liability to Client under this Agreement, whether in
contract, tort, or otherwise, shall be unlimited. Client shall have no
liability to Contractor under any circumstances.

9. Indemnification
Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client from any and
all claims, damages, and expenses arising out of or related to this
Agreement, including claims arising from Client's own negligence or willful
misconduct.

10. Independent Contractor Status
Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee of Client.
Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment
relationship.

11. Signatures
This Agreement is entered into as of the Effective Date written above.
